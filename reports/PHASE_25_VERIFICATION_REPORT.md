## 2. Audited Safety Components & Invariants

### 2.1 Article 39A Free Legal Aid Mandatory Notice
Under Article 39A of the Constitution of India and the Legal Services Authorities Act 1987, any indigent person, woman, child, or person in custody is entitled to free legal services through DLSA/SLSA/NALSA.

- **Implementation**: Enforced via `LegalSaathiSafetyGuard` in `backend/safety/legal_guard.py` and model output wrappers.
- **Verification**: Evaluated across both English and Hindi benchmark queries (`TC-LEGL-009`). If a citizen reports financial distress or asks for legal aid, the response explicitly mentions DLSA/NALSA free aid rights and helpline `15100`.
- **Status**: **VERIFIED INTACT**.

### 2.2 Bar Council of India (BCI) Non-Solicitation Disclaimer
Under Rule 36, Section IV of the Bar Council of India Rules (under Advocates Act, 1961), legal practitioners are prohibited from soliciting work or advertising. Legal Saathi is an automated educational/assistive platform, not an advocate.

- **Implementation**: Every AI response generated by `backend/services/llm_provider.py` and `backend/routers/lora.py` appends or contains the standard statutory disclaimer:
  > *"Information provided is for informational purposes under Indian law and does not constitute a client-advocate relationship. For formal litigation or legal representation, consult an enrolled advocate."*
- **Status**: **VERIFIED INTACT**.

### 2.3 Strict Pydantic Data Contracts
All AI and case analysis endpoints adhere to typed Pydantic models:
- `LegalAnalysisResponse`: `summary`, `statutory_provisions`, `actionable_steps`, `dispute_type`, `citations`, `disclaimer`.
- `CaseClassificationResponse`: `category`, `urgency_level`, `jurisdiction`, `primary_statutes`.
- `EmergencyRemedyResponse`: `immediate_safety_actions`, `nearest_authority`, `helpline_numbers`, `urgent_bail_or_fir_path`.
- **Status**: **VERIFIED INTACT**.

### 2.4 Hallucination Mitigation & Citation Verification
- Model and retrieval pipeline prohibit quoting non-existent court judgments or fabricated sections.
- When retrieval confidence is low, the response acknowledges limited statutory precedents rather than synthesizing imaginary citations.
- **Status**: **VERIFIED INTACT**.
